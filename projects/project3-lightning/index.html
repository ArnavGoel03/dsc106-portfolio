GLM counts total lightning flashes (in-cloud plus cloud-to-ground), so this page says flashes, not strikes. GLM detects a smaller share of flashes in daytime and near the edge of its view, so the curves are scaled to each cell's own peak and compare shapes, not raw totals. One summer of one year is a sample, not a climatology. Land outline: Natural Earth via world-atlas.
